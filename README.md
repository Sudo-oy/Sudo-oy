## 🧭 What I care about

- **Platforms as products**: reusable modules and golden paths instead of one-off scripts.
- **Security by default**: least privilege, no secrets in git, policy checks in every pipeline.
- **Operability**: good signals (metrics, alerts) and tooling that shortens incidents.
- **AI for operations, with guardrails**: read-only by design, grounded in evidence, human in the loop.
